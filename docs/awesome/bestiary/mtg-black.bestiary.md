# :lucide-sparkles: Magic: The Gathering — Black

Black mana: power at any cost. Reanimation, sacrifice, and creatures that thrive on death. If it dies, it fuels something worse.

!!! tip "Start Here"
    Black is the color of ambition and recursion. Start with Grave Titan, Sheoldred, and Gray Merchant to understand the color's identity.

---

## :lucide-skull: The Icons

### :lucide-skull: Grave Titan

**Origin:** Magic: The Gathering · **Type:** Zombie Giant · **Source:** Core Set 2011

A 6/6 deathtouch titan that spawns 2/2 Zombies on attack and on entry. The poster child for black's "value through death" philosophy — every trigger adds more fuel.

**Where to learn more:** [Scryfall: Grave Titan](https://scryfall.com/search?q=grave+titan) · [MTG Wiki: Grave Titan](https://mtg.fandom.com/wiki/Grave_Titan)

### :lucide-droplet: Sheoldred, the Apocalypse

**Origin:** Magic: The Gathering · **Type:** Phyrexian Praetor · **Source:** Dominaria United

A 4/5 deathtouch that drains opponents and heals you on every draw. The modern face of black: efficient, oppressive, and impossible to ignore.

**Where to learn more:** [Scryfall: Sheoldred](https://scryfall.com/search?q=sheoldred+the+apocalypse) · [MTG Wiki: Sheoldred](https://mtg.fandom.com/wiki/Sheoldred)

### :lucide-ghost: Gray Merchant of Asphodel

**Origin:** Magic: The Gathering · **Type:** Zombie · **Source:** Theros

"Gary" — a 2/4 that drains life equal to your black devotion on ETB. The budget black finisher that never stops being relevant.

**Where to learn more:** [Scryfall: Gray Merchant](https://scryfall.com/search?q=gray+merchant+of+asphodel)

## :material-fire: The Philosophy

Black converts death into advantage. Reanimation, sacrifice, and recursion are the tools; the cost is always life or resources. Removal is flexible, and few colors answer everything.

**Where to learn more:** [MTG Color Philosophy: Black](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/color-philosophy-black)
