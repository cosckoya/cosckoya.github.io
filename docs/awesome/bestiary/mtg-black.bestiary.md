# :lucide-sparkles: Magic: The Gathering — Black

Black mana: power at any cost. Reanimation, sacrifice, and creatures that thrive on death. If it dies, it fuels something worse.

!!! tip "2026 Update"
    Black remains the color of recursion and sacrifice. Commander staples rotate, but the philosophy stays: pay life, take lives, profit.

---

## Quick Hits

=== ":lucide-list-check: Essential Black"
    ```text
    # Iconic black creatures
    - Grave Titan
    - Sheoldred, the Apocalypse
    - Gray Merchant of Asphodel
    - Phyrexian Obliterator
    ```

    **Real talk:**
    - Black excels at recursion and removal.
    - Life is a resource; spend it.
    - Board wipes are a lifestyle.

=== ":lucide-bolt: Common Patterns"
    - **Reanimator**: dump big threats, bring them back cheap.
    - **Aristocrats**: sacrifice tokens for value.
    - **Drain**: chip damage that scales with board state.

    **Why this works:**
    - Black converts death into advantage.
    - Removal is flexible; few colors answer everything.

=== ":material-fire: Pro Tips & Gotchas"
    **Tips:**
    - Keep a sac outlet; value engines need fuel.
    - Pack graveyard hate against other black decks.

    **Gotchas:**
    - Overextending into board wipes.
    - Life loss adds up; track it.
