# :material-robot: Digimon

Digimon ("Digital Monsters") is Pokemon's edgier rival: creatures born from data in a digital world, who evolve — "digivolve" — through stages rather than species. The innovation is the evolution line: a single Digimon grows from a small Rookie into a godlike Mega, and the line itself is the story. Digimon are not collected; they are partners, and their evolution reflects the bond with their human partner. This page covers the most iconic evolution lines and villains.

!!! tip "Start Here"
    Digimon is organized by evolution stages: Fresh, In-Training, Rookie, Champion, Ultimate, Mega (and beyond). The classic line to know is Agumon's: Agumon → Greymon → MetalGreymon → WarGreymon. Digimon Wiki is the definitive reference. The original anime *Digimon Adventure* (1999) is the best entry point.

## :material-flame: The Classic Hero Lines

### :material-flame: Agumon → Greymon → MetalGreymon → WarGreymon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Reptile → Dinosaur → Cyborg → Dragon · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

The signature line of the franchise. Agumon is a small orange dinosaur that shoots fire ("Pepper Breath"); Greymon is a full Tyrannosaurus; MetalGreymon is a cyborg with a mechanical arm and chest cannon; WarGreymon is a dragon warrior in armor with the Dramon Destroyers on his arms. The line is the growth arc of Tai's partner, and it embodies Digimon's core idea: evolution as the relationship's strength.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Agumon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Agumon) · [Digimon Wiki: WarGreymon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/WarGreymon) · [Digimon Adventure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digimon_Adventure)

### :material-dog-side: Gabumon → Garurumon → WereGarurumon → MetalGarurumon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Reptile → Beast → Human-Beast → Cyborg · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

The cold counterpart to Agumon's fire: Gabumon is a small blue creature wearing a fur coat; Garurumon is a wolf; WereGarurumon is a wolf-man; MetalGarurumon is a mechanical wolf with missile launchers. It is Matt's partner, and the line embodies loyalty and the "cool" evolution. Together with WarGreymon it forms Omegamon — the fusion of fire and ice, the franchise's most iconic Mega.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Gabumon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Gabumon) · [Digimon Wiki: MetalGarurumon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/MetalGarurumon) · [Omegamon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Omegamon)

## :material-bird: The Supporting Lines

### :material-bird: Patamon → Angemon → HolyAngemon → Seraphimon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Mammal → Angel → Angel · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Patamon is the small, round, cute Rookie — and its evolution to Angemon is one of the franchise's most iconic moments, an angel with a golden staff who sacrifices himself in the fight against Devimon. The line then climbs to Seraphimon, the highest-ranking angel. It is the line of faith: the weakest-looking partner carrying the most divine power.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Patamon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Patamon) · [Digimon Wiki: Angemon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Angemon) · [The Devimon fight](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Devimon)

### :lucide-cat: Gatomon → Angewomon → Magnadramon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Beast → Angel → Dragon · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Gatomon is the cat Digimon who is secretly the reincarnation of Kari's partner — a Champion who appears early, hiding her true nature. Her evolution to Angewomon (the female angel with the bow of light) is the counterpart to Angemon, and together they form Magnadramon or Ophanimon. The line is the mystery: the underestimated creature revealing its divine origin.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Gatomon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Gatomon) · [Digimon Wiki: Angewomon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Angewomon) · [The 02 revelation](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Tailmon)

## :material-bug-outline: The Bugs & Machines

### :material-bug-outline: Tentomon → Kabuterimon → MegaKabuterimon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Insect → Insect → Cyborg insect · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Tentomon is the beetle Rookie, the partner of Izzy the brains of the group. Its line — Kabuterimon, a stag beetle; MegaKabuterimon, a massive beetle with a horn cannon — is the evolution of logic and analysis: the smallest of the group's partners becoming one of its most powerful. It is the line of the intellect.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Tentomon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Tentomon) · [Digimon Wiki: Kabuterimon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Kabuterimon) · [Izzy Izumi](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Izzy_Izumi)

### :material-robot: Andromon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Cyborg · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Andromon is a machine Digimon — a guardian robot with a chest cannon, often found protecting the servers of the digital world. It represents the machine layer of Digimon: beings that are pure data and circuitry, with no organic origin. Andromon is the monster as infrastructure: the guard you must pass to reach the core.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Andromon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Andromon) · [Machine Digimon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Machine) · [Server (Digital World)](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Server)

## :material-skull: The Villains

### :material-skull: Devimon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Fallen angel · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Devimon is the first great villain of the anime: a fallen angel Digimon who corrupts the digital world and turns the other Digimon against the children. He is the darkness inside the digital world — the opposite of Angemon, who sacrifices himself to defeat him. Devimon is the monster of the corrupted system: the angel who fell.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Devimon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Devimon) · [The File Island arc](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/File_Island) · [Dark Digimon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Evolution)

### :material-dna: Diaboromon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure: Our War Game!, 2000 · **Type:** Virus · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Diaboromon is the virus: a Digimon that multiplies through emails and the internet, threatening the real world from inside the network. The film *Our War Game!* — directed by Mamoru Hosoda, later of *Summer Wars* fame — is a masterpiece that anticipates digital-age anxiety. Diaboromon is the monster of the network: the code that went wrong and learned to hate.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Diaboromon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Diaboromon) · [Our War Game!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digimon_Adventure:_Our_War_Game!) · [Mamoru Hosoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamoru_Hosoda)

### :material-robot-dead: Machinedramon

**Origin:** Digimon Adventure, 1999 · **Type:** Machine dragon · **Source:** Bandai, Toei

Machinedramon is the ultimate machine Digimon — a city-destroying dragon of metal, the strongest of the Dark Masters in the later arc. It is the monster of pure military might: no soul, no mercy, just overwhelming firepower. It represents the nightmare of total automation.

**Where to learn more:** [Digimon Wiki: Machinedramon](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Machinedramon) · [The Dark Masters](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Masters) · [Digimon Adventure arc 2](https://digimon.fandom.com/wiki/Digimon_Adventure#Arc_2)
