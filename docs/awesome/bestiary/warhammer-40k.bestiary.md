# :lucide-skull: Warhammer 40K — Black Legion

The Black Legion: traitor Astartes united under Abaddon. Black and gold armor, terror tactics, and the Long War.

!!! tip "Start Here"
    Start with Abaddon, Chosen, and Obliterators — the core of most Black Legion lists.

---

## :lucide-shield: The Legion

### :lucide-crown: Abaddon the Despoiler

**Origin:** Warhammer 40K · **Type:** Chaos Lord · **Source:** Codex: Chaos Space Marines

The Warmaster of Chaos, wielding Drach'nyen and the Talon of Horus. A force multiplier and the face of the Black Legion.

**Where to learn more:** [Lexicanum: Abaddon](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Abaddon_the_Despoiler) · [Wahapedia](https://wahapedia.ru)

### :lucide-sword: Chosen / Legionaries

**Origin:** Warhammer 40K · **Type:** Infantry · **Source:** Codex: Chaos Space Marines

Veteran traitors with flexible loadouts. The backbone of Black Legion lists.

**Where to learn more:** [Lexicanum: Chosen](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Chosen)

### :lucide-zap: Obliterators

**Origin:** Warhammer 40K · **Type:** Heavy support · **Source:** Codex: Chaos Space Marines

Daemon-infused heavy weapons platforms. Versatile firepower, terrifying presence.

**Where to learn more:** [Lexicanum: Obliterator](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Obliterator)

### :lucide-flame: Heldrake / Defiler

**Origin:** Warhammer 40K · **Type:** Daemon engine · **Source:** Codex: Chaos Space Marines

Flying or walking daemon engines for fire support and terror. Black Legion loves these.

**Where to learn more:** [Lexicanum: Heldrake](https://wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Heldrake)

## :material-fire: Tactics

Black Legion is a toolbox army: mix marks, units, and daemon engines. Keep Abaddon central for auras; manage CP carefully.
