# :lucide-skull: Warhammer 40K — Black Legion

The Black Legion: traitor Astartes united under Abaddon. Black and gold armor, terror tactics, and the Long War.

!!! tip "2026 Update"
    Black Legion remains a flexible Chaos Space Marines army. Vectors of attack and varied warbands keep it adaptable.

---

## Quick Hits

=== ":lucide-list-check: Essential Units"
    - Chaos Terminators
    - Chosen / Legionaries
    - Obliterators
    - Heldrake / Defiler
    - Abaddon the Despoiler

    **Real talk:**
    - Black Legion is a toolbox army.
    - Marks of Chaos add flavor and buffs.

=== ":lucide-bolt: Common Patterns"
    - **Despoiler's Wreath**: aggressive push with re-rolls.
    - **Veterans of the Long War**: seasoned traitors with buffs.
    - **Daemon engines** for fire support.

    **Why this works:**
    - Flexible detachments; mix marks and units.
    - Strong HQ in Abaddon.

=== ":material-fire: Pro Tips & Gotchas"
    **Tips:**
    - Keep Abaddon central for auras.
    - Use terrain to protect fragile units.

    **Gotchas:**
    - Chaos can be CP-hungry.
    - Morale and leadership matter; don't overextend.
