# :lucide-sparkles: Anime & Manga

Japanese pop culture is the direct heir of the yokai tradition — and it took the monster and ran. Anime and manga gave us some of the most iconic monsters of the 21st century: the Titans of *Attack on Titan*, the Hollows of *Bleach*, the Curses of *Jujutsu Kaisen*, the Apostles of *Berserk*. These creatures inherit the yokai's rules — shapeshifting, masks, the supernatural hidden in the everyday — and add modern anxieties: war, trauma, identity, and the body.

!!! tip "Start Here"
    The four essential anime monster universes: *Attack on Titan* (Titans — the monster as wall-breaker), *Bleach* (Hollows — the monster as lost soul), *Jujutsu Kaisen* (Curses — the monster as negative emotion), and *Berserk* (Apostles — the monster as ambition). Each one treats the monster differently, and together they cover the range.

## :material-skull: Attack on Titan

### :material-skull: The Titans

**Origin:** Attack on Titan, 2009 (manga) / 2013 (anime) · **Type:** Giant humanoids · **Source:** Hajime Isayama

Titans are 3-to-15 meter tall humanoids that exist only to eat humans — they have no need to eat, no reproductive drive, and are biologically immortal. They appear after a massive walled civilization, and the horror is double: the walls that protect humanity are made of them, and the truth of their origin reshapes the entire story. The Titans are the monster as apocalypse: the wall you live behind is the enemy you fear.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Titans (AoT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titans_(Attack_on_Titan)) · [Attack on Titan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attack_on_Titan) · [Hajime Isayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajime_Isayama)

## :material-ghost: Bleach

### :material-ghost: Hollows

**Origin:** Bleach, 2001 (manga) / 2004 (anime) · **Type:** Corrupted souls · **Source:** Tite Kubo

Hollows are souls that failed to pass on — humans who died with regret or malice and transformed into masked monsters that devour other souls. Each hollow wears a white mask, a symbol of the face it lost. The strongest hollows evolve into Arrancars, who break their masks and gain humanoid form and sword powers. Hollows are the monster as trauma: the grief that becomes a predator.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Hollows (Bleach)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_(Bleach)) · [Bleach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bleach_(manga)) · [Tite Kubo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tite_Kubo)

## :material-dna: Jujutsu Kaisen

### :material-dna: Curses

**Origin:** Jujutsu Kaisen, 2018 (manga) / 2020 (anime) · **Type:** Negative emotion spirits · **Source:** Gege Akutami

Curses are born from humanity's negative emotions — fear, hatred, grief — and they grow stronger the more people feel them. They range from low-grade pests to Special Grade monsters like Mahito, the curse of human hatred, or Sukuna, the King of Curses, a fragment of a primordial entity. Curses are the monster as emotion: the more you hate, the more you feed them. The manga's fights are among the most celebrated in modern shonen.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Jujutsu Kaisen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jujutsu_Kaisen) · [Cursed spirits](https://jujutsu-kaisen.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Spirits) · [Gege Akutami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gege_Akutami)

## :material-sword-cross: Berserk

### :material-sword-cross: The Apostles

**Origin:** Berserk, 1989 (manga) · **Type:** Demonic transformed humans · **Source:** Kentaro Miura

Apostles are humans who sacrificed what they loved most to become demons — the servants of the God Hand, the five godlike beings who rule the astral plane. The transformation is the series' central horror: in the Eclipse, Griffith sacrifices his band of followers and is reborn as Femto, the fifth member of the God Hand. Apostles are the monster as ambition: the price of power is the soul, and the soul is real in Berserk.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Berserk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berserk_(manga)) · [God Hand](https://berserk.fandom.com/wiki/God_Hand) · [Kentaro Miura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentaro_Miura)

## :material-robot: The Modern Icons

### :material-robot: Angels (Neon Genesis Evangelion)

**Origin:** Neon Genesis Evangelion, 1995 · **Type:** Cosmic beings · **Source:** Hideaki Anno

The Angels are the beings that attack Tokyo-3 in *Evangelion* — cosmic creatures with an AT Field (Absolute Terror Field) that only Evangelion units can break. Each Angel has a unique form: the giant Sachiel, the arm-bladed Ramiel (a perfect octahedron), the emaciated Leliel. They are the monster as existential dread: the things that come from beyond, and the trauma of the children forced to fight them.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Angels (Evangelion)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angel_(Neon_Genesis_Evangelion)) · [Neon Genesis Evangelion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neon_Genesis_Evangelion) · [Hideaki Anno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hideaki_Anno)

### :material-fire: The Tailed Beasts (Naruto)

**Origin:** Naruto, 1999 (manga) · **Type:** Chakra monsters · **Source:** Masashi Kishimoto

The Tailed Beasts are nine colossal creatures of chakra, each with a number of tails — from Shukaku the one-tailed tanuki to Kurama the nine-tailed fox. They are sealed inside humans (jinchuriki) and used as weapons of war. The story reframes them: Kurama, initially the monster within, becomes a partner and a friend. The Tailed Beasts are the monster as weapon: the power you carry inside, for good or ill.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Tailed Beasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tailed_Beasts_(Naruto)) · [Naruto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naruto) · [Masashi Kishimoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masashi_Kishimoto)

### :lucide-fish: The Sea Kings (One Piece)

**Origin:** One Piece, 1997 (manga) · **Type:** Sea monsters · **Source:** Eiichiro Oda

The Sea Kings are the monsters of the Grand Line's oceans in *One Piece* — colossal sea creatures that can swallow ships, ruled by the ancient sea kings who answer the call of the Voice of All Things. They are the monsters of the open sea: the reason the world's greatest sailors fear the water more than each other.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: One Piece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Piece) · [Sea Kings](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Sea_King) · [Eiichiro Oda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiichiro_Oda)

## :lucide-ghost: The Yokai Heirs

### :lucide-ghost: The Demons of Demon Slayer

**Origin:** Demon Slayer, 2016 (manga) / 2019 (anime) · **Type:** Vampiric demons · **Source:** Koyoharu Gotouge

The demons of *Demon Slayer* are humans transformed by Muzan Kibutsuji, the first demon — vampires who cannot survive sunlight, feed on humans, and each has a unique Blood Demon Art. The horror is personal: the demons are often tragic, and the hero Tanjiro's sister Nezuko is one of them. The demons are the monster as family: the disease that takes the people you love.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Demon Slayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demon_Slayer:_Kimetsu_no_Yaiba) · [Muzan Kibutsuji](https://kimetsu-no-yaiba.fandom.com/wiki/Muzan_Kibutsuji) · [Koyoharu Gotouge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koyoharu_Gotouge)

### :material-bug-outline: The Chimera Ants (Hunter x Hunter)

**Origin:** Hunter x Hunter, 1998 (manga) · **Type:** Hybrid creatures · **Source:** Yoshihiro Togashi

The Chimera Ants are a species that eats other creatures and incorporates their traits into their offspring — creating a queen and her army of hybrid soldiers, culminating in the king, Meruem. The Chimera Ant arc is one of the greatest story arcs in manga history, and its monsters are the horror of evolution: nature optimizing itself past humanity.

**Where to learn more:** [Wikipedia: Hunter x Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_%C3%97_Hunter) · [Chimera Ants](https://hunterxhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Chimera_Ants) · [Yoshihiro Togashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoshihiro_Togashi)
